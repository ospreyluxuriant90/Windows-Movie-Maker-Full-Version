## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Create Movies** — Craft your own movies or animations using photos and videos.
- **Import Media** — Easily import videos, images, and audio files.
- **Add Music** — Incorporate music from your library or online services.
- **Record Video** — Capture video from a webcam and narrate with a microphone.
- **Timeline Editing** — Drag and drop to modify the sequence of frames.

- **Image Effects** — Apply effects, transitions, and subtitles to enhance your videos.
- **Credits** — Add opening or closing credits to highlight contributors.
- **Preview Videos** — Preview your edits before finalizing them.
- **Cloud Upload** — Save creations locally or upload them to the cloud.
